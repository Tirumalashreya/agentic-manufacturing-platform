# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. This client has already rejected proposals that felt cliché or templated, and is paying for a distinctive point of view: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief, and take aesthetic risk if justified.

## Avoid the Tells of AI-Generated Design:
1. The SaaS-card kit: content chopped into identical rounded cards, one border-radius on everything regardless of hierarchy, the same soft grey shadow under each, and gradient washes as decoration.
2. Template chrome: tracked-out ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels above every heading; meta strings joined with middle dots ('A · B · C'); labels built as 'WORD — fragment' with a spaced em dash; tinted near-black (#0B0B0B, #111) standing in for black; a monospace face for small data labels; a '→' appended to link and button text.
3. Accenting just a single word or phrase in a headline.
4. Using all caps for labels.
5. Adding unnecessary typographic labels above content.

## Key Principles:
- Spend your boldness in one place: Let one element be the memorable thing, keep everything around it quiet and disciplined.
- Typography carries personality: Use one or two deliberate typefaces with intentional weights, widths, and spacing.
- Structural devices (outlines, borders, numbering) encode useful information rather than decorate.
- Write from the end user's perspective in simple, active terms.
